About Me

I am a Surrey-based undergraduate student of SFU's Interactive Arts and Technology program. The primary goal of the work I do as a game developer is effectively working in lockstep with the teams of artists, designers and other creatives: brainstorming, programming and problem solving to bring novel gameplay experiences that resonate with people. I believe that my job is not done until the game I am working on is backed by successful playtesting, and confirmation that the company feels their ambitions have been embodied by my work.

In my spare time, I have been developing a turn-based Role-Playing game with custom graphics and sprites, studying tarot cards, and nerding out with my family and friends about music of a variety of genres! Another thing about me is that I enjoy working and gaming in the early night, which is why there is a moon on my logo!
